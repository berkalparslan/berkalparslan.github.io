Ne yapıyor?What it does

Fazlası yok. Bir işi iyi yapsın diye yazıldı.

Nothing more than this. It was built to do one job well.

Colour, dot style, frame and a logo in the middle. Corporate or playful, both work.

Codes you make and scan are kept, so you never rebuild the same wifi code twice.

The code on your wrist is enough to get you through the door.
